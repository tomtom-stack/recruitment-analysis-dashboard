# Recruitment Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

A Power BI recruitment analytics dashboard built to analyse hiring outcomes, candidate characteristics, and recruitment strategy effectiveness.

The project uses a dataset of 1,500 candidates to explore patterns in hiring decisions and identify candidate and recruitment factors associated with better hiring outcomes.

## Business Questions

- What percentage of candidates were hired?
- Does education level appear to influence hiring decisions?
- Does experience level affect hiring outcomes?
- Which recruitment strategy has the highest hiring success?
- How do interview scores differ between hired and non-hired candidates?
- How do skill scores differ between hired and non-hired candidates?
- How do personality scores differ between hired and non-hired candidates?
- Does distance from the company appear related to hiring decisions?
- Does previous company show any pattern in hiring?
- Are there noticeable differences in hiring outcomes by gender and age?
- What candidate and recruitment factors are associated with better hiring outcomes?

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of recruitment performance, including hiring rate, recruitment strategy performance, and candidate education patterns.

### 2. Candidate Analysis
Examines hiring outcomes across age, gender, experience level, and overall candidate hiring decisions.

### 3. Hiring Effectiveness
Analyzes assessment scores, distance from the company, and previous-company patterns in relation to hiring outcomes.

### 4. Hiring Insights
Uses Power BI Key Influencers to identify candidate and recruitment factors associated with the Hired outcome.

## Key Findings

- The Aggressive recruitment strategy showed the strongest hiring association in the Key Influencers analysis.
- Skill score was the strongest assessment-related factor surfaced by the Key Influencers analysis.
- Personality and interview scores also showed positive associations with hiring outcomes.
- Education level and experience appeared among the factors associated with hiring outcomes.
- The analysis highlights patterns and associations rather than causal relationships.
